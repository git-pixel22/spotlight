Spotlight never writes for you.

An editor with a grease pencil picks the frame. They don't repaint it. Spotlight works the same way with what you say.

  • Noticesthe moments in your photos worth sharing.
  • Asksone specific question about each.
  • Tidiesthe ums, and trims to fit each network.
  • Neverinvents facts, prices or claims. Never puts on a marketing voice.

The $0 stack
