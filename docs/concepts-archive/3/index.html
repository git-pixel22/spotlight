Spotlight never writes for you.

It notices, it asks and it tidies. The post is what you said, without the ums.

  • Noticesthe moments in your week that are worth sharing.
  • Asksone specific question about each.
  • Tidiesthe filler, and fits each network's character limit.
  • InventsNever. No made-up facts, prices or claims.
  • SellsNever. No marketing voice.

The kind of thing it asks

  • Which flavour disappears first?
  • Who's this regular?
  • Black Friday in 15 days: are you doing anything, or deliberately skipping it?
