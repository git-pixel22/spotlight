How it works

Three steps, about two minutes. Follow one photo, a box of cookies from a home baker's camera roll, all the way into her Buffer queue.

  1. 1

    Drop the week's photos

    The ones already on your phone. Messy ones included.

    Six photos go in. Keep an eye on the cookie box.

  2. 2

    Spotlight notices, and asks

    It picks the 3 to 5 moments worth sharing and asks one specific question about each. You answer out loud.

    The cookie box is one of them.

    Spotlight asks

    Which flavour disappears first?

    0:10

    “Um so matcha Oreo, every single time. I almost didn't make it like because I thought matcha was too niche, you know…”

  3. 3

    Your own words land in Buffer

    One post per channel, fitted to each network's limits. Saved as a draft, queued, or kept as an Idea.

Spotlight never writes for you

The post is what you said. Spotlight takes out the ums, fits it to each network, and stops there.

  • It noticesthe moments in your week worth sharing.
  • It asksone specific question about each.
  • It tidiesfiller words and character limits.
  • It neverinvents facts, prices or claims, or puts on a marketing voice.
