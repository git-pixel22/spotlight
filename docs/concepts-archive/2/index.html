Spotlight never writes for you.

It takes out the ums and fits the character limit. Everything else is exactly what you said.

  • No invented factsNo prices, claims or details you didn't say. If you didn't mention it, it isn't in the post.
  • No marketing voiceNo "we're thrilled to announce". You sound like you on a good day, because it is you.
  • No blank pageYou never start from an empty caption box. You start from a question you can answer.
