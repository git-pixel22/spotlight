How it works

Follow one moment the whole way. A potter, a bowl, and eleven seconds of talking.

  1. 1

    Drop the week's photos

    The ones already on your phone. Blurry, messy and half-finished ones included.

    Spotlight picks 3 to 5 worth sharing. This bowl is one.

  2. 2

    It asks. You answer out loud.

    One specific question per moment. Talk for ten seconds, the way you'd tell a customer.

    Who's this bowl for?
    0:11

    "Um, so this one's for Maya. She, like, orders one every year for her mum's birthday. This is the sixth, you know, and I still get nervous trimming the foot."

  3. 3

    Your words land in Buffer

    One post per channel, fitted to each network's limit. Draft it, queue it, or keep it as an Idea.

    In your Buffer3 channels

Spotlight never writes for you

It works the light. You say the lines. What reaches Buffer is what came out of your mouth, minus the ums.

What it does

  • Notices the moment
  • Asks one question
  • Takes out "um", "like" and "you know"
  • Fits each network's character limit

What it never does

  • Invent a fact, a price or a claim
  • Put on a marketing voice

What you said a home baker, 10 seconds

"Um so matcha Oreo, every single time. I almost didn't make it like because I thought matcha was too niche, you know, and now people message me just to ask if it's in the box."
